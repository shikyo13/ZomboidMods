Thanks for the feedback. Current B42.18 verified code only applies BHZ damage while PZ reports the zombie in ThumpState with a real thump target, or AttackVehicleState against a player in a vehicle, and BHZ can observe a real vanilla hit result. Structure hits require that zombie's thump condition to decrease. Vehicle hits require real window health or part condition loss. There is no gameplay cooldown fallback.

Default math: wood 0.0500 HP/hit = 20 hits per 1.0 HP; heavy metal 0.0700 = about 15 hits; vehicle metal 0.0625 = 16 hits. My SP debug run matched the math, but reports suggest some configs/mod combos may differ.

Please enable BHZ DebugMode=true, VehicleDebugMode=false, LogLevel=5, reproduce, close PZ, and attach %USERPROFILE%\Zomboid\Lua\BHZDebug.log here:
[url=https://github.com/shikyo13/ZomboidMods/issues]GitHub issues[/url]

The log includes PZ build, SP/MP side, BHZ settings, ZombieLore, active mods, hit-signal lines, and each damage tick.
