Thanks for the report! The mod is designed to damage zombies when they thump structures (doors, windows, barricades, fences) and vehicles. A few questions to help me investigate:

1. What type of fences are you seeing this on - tall fences, barricaded fences, or regular yard fences?
2. Have you changed any sandbox settings for the mod (Base Damage, Damage Mode, etc.)?
3. What PZ version are you on?

In the meantime, you can lower the "Base Damage" in sandbox settings (default is 5%) if zombies are dying too quickly. Setting it to 1-2% will slow things down significantly.
