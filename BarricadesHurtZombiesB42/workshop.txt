description=A comprehensive Build 42 update that makes zombies take damage when attacking barricades, doors, windows, and vehicles. This mod introduces damage systems that make fortifications matter in the zombie apocalypse.
description=
description=[h2]Key Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Damage on thumpable objects such as doors, windows, and barricades.
description=[*]Fully functional vehicle damage system based on B42 vehicle attack states
description=[*]Material-based damage scaling for both barricades and vehicles
description=[*]SVU3 armor script and visible armor part detection when available
description=[*]Customizable sandbox settings for all damage values
description=[*]Visual feedback through blood effects
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]How It Works[/h2]
description=When zombies attack structures or vehicles, they take damage based on what they are attacking. The system identifies different structure materials and vehicle armor signals:
description=
description=[b]Material Damage Multipliers:[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]Wood Structures: Base damage (1.0x)
description=[*]Metal Structures: 25% more damage (1.25x)
description=[*]Heavy Metal: 40% more damage (1.4x)
description=[*]Light Spikes: 50% more damage (1.5x) - SVU3 armor script/part support
description=[*]Heavy Spikes: 75% more damage (1.75x) - SVU3 armor script/part support
description=[*]Reinforced: Double damage (2.0x) - SVU3 armor script/part support
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Vehicle System[/h2]
description=The mod features a vehicle damage system that:
description=[list]
description=[*]Detects B42 vehicle attack state against occupied vehicles
description=[*]Uses the passenger door, window, or nearest bodywork part when B42 exposes one for the attack
description=[*]Skips damage safely when B42 exposes no real vehicle window or part damage signal
description=[*]Applies appropriate damage multipliers based on material type
description=[/list]

description=[h2]B42.18 Status[/h2]
description=This update has been checked against the locally installed Project Zomboid 42.18.0 Unstable files. Core BHZ assumptions still match the current jar: OnZombieUpdate timing, vanilla ThumpFrame internals, zombie owner APIs, AttackVehicleState, and zombie health sync are still present.
description=
description=No damage value tuning changed in v2.2.4. BHZ no longer calls ActionContext.hasEventOccurred from Lua because runtime testing showed that API can raise Lua errors even inside pcall. Structure damage now requires the zombie's vanilla thump condition to decrease for that zombie and target. Vehicle damage now requires real vehicle window health or vehicle part condition loss, with shared part de-duping so one vehicle hit is not applied to every nearby attacking zombie. If a required hit signal is unavailable, BHZ skips damage and logs the failure instead of approximating hits with timer-based damage. Material detection and MP RPC ownership validation are unchanged from v2.2.3. Dedicated MP remains best-effort while B42 is on the unstable branch.
description=

description=[*]Please also mention what looked wrong in-game, such as blood with no damage, full-health zombies suddenly dying, or the object/vehicle being thumped
